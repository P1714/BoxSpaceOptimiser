⚠️Residual risks and release gates

These items require operator or carrier review and are not solved by the cuboid packing model.

Exact-limit stack
Sofa H-013 carries two wardrobe boxes at 20 kg each: 40 kg against its 40 kg limit. Zero modeled margin. Obtain supervisor confirmation or repack and rerun validation.
  • Axle-level load distribution and centre of gravity.
  • Load restraints, lashing, and dynamic stability under braking/cornering.
  • Real packaging dimensions, cushioning, and irregular shapes.
  • Rear-door access, loading/unloading sequence, manoeuvrability, and delivery order.
  • Real trip quotes and operating costs are not supplied by the organizer.
  • Heuristic search does not prove that a single-truck plan is impossible or that the selected fleet is globally optimal.

📚Source and assumptions

Inventory dimensions, quantities, weights, handling rules and vehicle specifications are copied from the organizer-provided Excel workbook. The business objectives and stated constraints come from the six-page BoxSpaceOptimiser case PDF. Item volume, utilization and payload metrics are calculated from those inputs. Relative price ratios are illustrative assumptions carried from the final solution workbook; no external numeric industry benchmark or actual transport quote is used in this prototype.

This is an interactive visualization of saved plan data. It does not run the packing engine or recalculate physical feasibility when an item is moved. Any plan changes must be produced by the optimization workflow and independently revalidated before use.
